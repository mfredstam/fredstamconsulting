What I Do

What I offer

  • Embedded Software Engineering

    Embedded software that runs for decades, not just demos — flight computers, ECUs and other real-time systems. I design and implement in C, C++ and Rust across bare-metal, RTOS, embedded Linux and AUTOSAR, for products in regulated industries.

  • Safety-Critical Software

    DO-178C avionics and ISO 26262 automotive software, developed with the evidence to prove it — traceability, requirements-based testing, coverage analysis, verification. So certification never becomes your project's critical path.
