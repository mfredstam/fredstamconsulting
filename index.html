What I Do

Services

Whether you need a specialist to lead a certification effort, a security review, or fill a critical gap in your team's compliance knowledge — Fredstam Consulting integrates with your team and delivers.

  • Software Engineering & Development

    Embedded and real-time software development in C, C++, and Rust, with RTOS expertise across bare-metal and multi-threaded systems. The engineering foundation that makes compliance achievable — written to the standards your certification demands.

  • Safety Certification Support

    End-to-end support for software certification under DO-178C and ISO 26262 across Aerospace and Automotive. Rigorous process adherence, traceability, and verification — so your team reaches the highest integrity levels without rework.

  • Cybersecurity Compliance

    Security engineering for embedded and connected systems aligned with IEC 62443 and ISO 21434 — threat modeling (STRIDE/TARA), secure coding, and vulnerability analysis. Building regulatory-ready resilience into your product from the ground up.
